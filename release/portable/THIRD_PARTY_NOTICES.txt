FFmpeg
------

UEFN-Ducky bundles FFmpeg (ffmpeg.exe, ffprobe.exe and shared DLLs) in tools/ffmpeg/,
together with its LICENSE.txt. It is used to extract frames and audio from chat videos.

- License: LGPL-2.1-or-later (BtbN win64-lgpl-shared build; no GPL components).
- Build: BtbN autobuild-2026-08-31-13-27 (FFmpeg n9.0.1), unmodified.
- The libraries are shared DLLs beside the executables, so you may replace them with your
  own build of the same FFmpeg version.
- UEFN-Ducky runs FFmpeg only as a separate process and does not link against it.
- Source: https://ffmpeg.org and https://github.com/BtbN/FFmpeg-Builds
